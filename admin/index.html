Reset your password

Your admin password is saved in your Vercel settings. Set a new one there:

  1. Log in to vercel.com. If you can't, use Vercel's own "Forgot password" or sign in with GitHub.
  2. Open your waithaka_steev project, then Settings → Environment Variables.
  3. Find ADMIN_PASSWORD, click ⋯ → Edit, type a new password and click Save.
  4. Go to Deployments. On the top one, click ⋯ → Redeploy, then confirm.
  5. Wait about a minute, then come back here and log in with the new password.
