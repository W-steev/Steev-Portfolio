About me

I turn ideas into working websites

I'm Stephen Waithaka, but everyone calls me Steev. I'm a web developer in Nyahururu with a background in science education from Mount Kenya University. I love building things people can actually use, from business websites to full online shops with carts, customer accounts, order tracking and M-Pesa checkout.

I also build hardware. My Lemark Smart Drainage System, made with an ESP32 and several sensors, reached the national level of the Kenya Science and Engineering Fair (KSEF) in 2026. If you want to automate a DIY project, I'm your plug.
